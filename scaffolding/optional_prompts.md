## PS1 Scaffolding Companion

Optional prompts and examples to help you develop the proposal and give useful feedback. Use the Assignment and Review Guide for requirements; these prompts are not extra tasks or form fields.

## Start with the class example

The supplied LLM strategic reasoning proposal is the imagined Jia et al. (NeurIPS 2025) proposal used in class. Its core question is how reasoning depth, decision noise and social context jointly explain LLM choices. The sample ACM version keeps that study and adds the class submission structure, notes and roadmap.

The planned 22-model, 13-setting, 30-trial design belongs to this reconstruction of the paper. It is not a required scale for your homework. The optional notebook and game illustrate a small synthetic case; they do not reproduce the full paper or measure actual LLM behavior.

## Connect the three disciplines

Economics asks whether a departure from equilibrium reflects limited depth or noisy responses to incentives. Computer science asks how to evaluate recursive strategic reasoning and computational effort. Behavioral science asks how human-like reasoning limits and demographic framing affect choice. What answer becomes possible only when these perspectives work together?

## Make the method checkable

Which players, strategies, payoffs, information and timing matter? Which solution concept provides a benchmark, and what is its defining source? Can a reader reproduce one calculation and change one parameter? What baseline or alternative explanation would challenge your interpretation?

## Explain evidence honestly

In the class example, TQRE estimates reasoning depth and decision precision from choices. Those estimates are model-dependent, not direct observations of cognition. Would uncertainty, a different game, repeated sampling or a competing model change the conclusion? Expected findings belong in future tense until measured.

## Optional prompts for reviewers and authors

## Appreciation

What is the strongest question, insight, argument or design choice? Where do the disciplines meaningfully interact? What does the figure, code or demonstration make especially clear? Name the passage or behavior that supports your appreciation.

Example: “The distinction between depth and decision noise gives the proposal a clear economic question, and Figure 1 explains why the behavioral model is needed.”

## Constructive criticism

Which concern most affects the interpretation? Is the issue an error, missing evidence, an unclear assumption or an overextended claim? What is the smallest feasible revision that would address it? Separate essential work from an optional extension.

Example: “The proposal treats an estimated depth as a cognitive fact. Please qualify the interpretation and show whether another depth–noise combination gives similar choices in the pilot.”

## Questions for scientific communication

Which term, symbol or connection would a reader from another discipline struggle to follow? Does the text distinguish a plan from a result? Is the contribution beyond the cited foundation explicit? Can the author explain one key mechanism more concretely?

Example: “What does precision mean in ordinary language, and how would a student see its effect in the interactive game?”

## A useful author response

“I agree that the original wording overstated identification. Section 3 now calls depth a model-dependent estimate. Appendix A adds a sensitivity check, and notebook cell 7 records its result. The broader human-validation study remains future work.” This illustrates the form of a response; it is not a claim that this check has been performed.

## Optional scaffolding for the roadmap and statements

## Connect foundations with the 2056 ambition

Name a laureate, cite the defining work and the award record, and state what that foundation explains. Which enduring human question remains? What is distinctive about today’s agents, institutions, information or technology? What specific new economic, computational and behavioral contribution would advance the boundary?

Use milestones that lead from a PS1 baseline to a discriminating study, broader validation and reusable theory or tools. Say what evidence would count as progress and what would require changing direction. Avoid treating an unsolved question as proof that earlier laureates failed.
