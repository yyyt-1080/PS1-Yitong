Campus Exchange Trust Game

A minimal decision-under-uncertainty demo: does an AI-verification badge change the counterpart's likelihood of reciprocating a trust decision?



 

You are the Requester. Each round you choose to trust an anonymous counterpart on the platform (send the item). The counterpart's response is generated by a fixed, illustrative probability encoded for this condition — it is not measured from real users.

Static Hugging Face Space: client-side HTML/CSS/JavaScript only. No Hugging Face CPU/GPU runtime, backend, database, API key, secret, or paid service is used. All computation (the seeded random-number generator and the round outcomes) runs locally in your browser. Reciprocation probabilities (0.75 / 0.45) are theoretical placeholders motivated by Akerlof (1970), Morozov & Feigel (2026), and Leung et al. (2026) — they are not empirical estimates of real user behavior. Any claim about how real campus users would behave under these two conditions is anticipated, not obtained.
