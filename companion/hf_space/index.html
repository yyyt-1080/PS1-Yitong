Model, evidence, and research connection

This is a simplified truncated logit cognitive hierarchy. Level 0 mixes uniformly; level k responds to a normalized Poisson mixture of lower levels. Precision is γk. We retain levels 0–16 and renormalize. The model is neither a Nash solver nor a quantal-response-equilibrium fixed point.

Inspired by Jia et al., NeurIPS 2025, with the baseline matrix from Table 1(b), and Camerer, Ho & Chong (2004). This original teaching adaptation does not reproduce the paper’s LLM evaluation. No real LLM calls or human research data are used.
